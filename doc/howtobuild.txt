BE AWARE: The "sandbox" directories (wba\sdgit\sd\src and doc) are NOT
plain directories that I have complete ownership of, and that Git
monitors and that I use to transmit stuff to Git.  They are windows
onto my branch.  The branch files themselves are legitimately on my
disk, but the wba\sdgit directories are windows into same, that Git
can alter.  The windows are selected by the "current branch" in Git.
The branch is selected by the "git switch" command.  The branch
selected by "git switch" and by the web do not affect each other.

THEREFORE: Do no do ANYTHING without checking (git status will show
it) that the branch is correct and that the files in wba\sdgit are the
expected ones.

   and once in the "sd-regression" directory.
